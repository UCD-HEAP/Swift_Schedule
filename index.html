Swift Observations of Interest to VERITAS

Page last updated: UT 2026-10-23 23:50

The following table contains scheduled Swift observations (PPST) of objects that are of interest to VERITAS for the current and following UT Dates. Note: as flown (AFST) may be different!

Swift observations are selected for targets within 0.1 deg. of objects in the VERITAS database.

Observations where the sun is > -18 deg. elevation at either the start or end of the Swift observation are flagged wth a ☼ symbol.

Items in red are Swift observations that have already happened (end time in the past).
